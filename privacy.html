Privacy Policy

Take Salam from Walton Lift R&I ("the app") is an internal tool used by Walton employees to log and track lift research, installation, and support activity.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from your Google account. This is used only to identify you within the app and to route your account to a Superadmin for approval.

How it's used

Your name and email are shown to Admins and Superadmins for account approval and internal reporting purposes only. Task data you log (categories, notes, lift IDs, etc.) is visible to your team according to the app's role permissions.

What we don't do

We do not sell, share, or use your data for advertising. We do not access your Gmail inbox, contacts, files, or any Google data beyond your basic name and email.

Data storage

Data is stored securely in a Supabase database used exclusively for this internal tool.
